Write your sitcom, from cold open to tag.

SitCraft is a desktop app for sitcom writers. Plan episodes with proven sitcom structure, lay them out on a timeline, have Claude draft the script in industry format, and keep a show bible that remembers every character, location and running gag — episode by episode.

Episode Lab

Describe an episode and get a beat-by-beat structure with A, B and C plots.

Timeline & outline

See every scene across the cold open, three acts and tag.

Script editor

Industry-standard formatting, revision colours, checks and Fountain export.

Living show bible

Characters' voices, likes, history and continuity — updated from each finished episode.

Your work stays yours

Everything is saved on your computer. Optional backups go to your own Google Drive.

Bring your own Claude key

SitCraft uses your Anthropic API key, so you control your usage and costs.

Google Drive backup

If you choose to sign in with Google, SitCraft saves copies of your show to a backups folder in your own Google Drive. SitCraft can only see and manage the files it creates there — never the rest of your Drive. Read the privacy policy for details.
