Privacy policy

Effective October 7, 2026

SitCraft is a desktop application for writing sitcoms. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have. In short: your work lives on your computer, SitCraft has no servers of its own, and nothing is collected about you.

What stays on your computer

  • Your shows and scripts. Episodes, outlines, timelines, scripts, the show bible and settings are saved in a data file in your Documents folder. Local backup copies are kept in the same place.
  • Your Anthropic API key. If you add one, it is stored on your computer, encrypted with your operating system's built-in protection (on Windows, Data Protection API). It is only ever sent to Anthropic.
  • Your Google sign-in. If you connect Google Drive, the sign-in token is stored on your computer, encrypted the same way.

What is sent, and only when you ask

  • To Anthropic (Claude). When you use features that ask Claude to write or analyse — such as generating an episode structure, creating a script or updating the show bible — the text needed for that request (for example your episode description, show bible details and script) is sent to Anthropic's API using your own API key. Anthropic's handling of that data is covered by Anthropic's privacy policy and the terms of your Anthropic account.
  • To Google (Google Drive). Only if you choose to sign in with Google: backup files of your show are uploaded to a folder in your own Google Drive.

SitCraft does not include analytics, advertising or tracking, and does not send your data anywhere else.

Google user data

If you sign in with Google, SitCraft requests only these permissions:

  • See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files that SitCraft creates (drive.file). SitCraft uses this to create its backup folder, upload backup files, download a backup when you choose to restore it, and delete a backup when you ask it to. SitCraft cannot see any other files in your Google Drive.
  • Your email address and basic sign-in identity (openid, email). SitCraft uses your email address only to show you which Google account is connected.

Google user data is used only to provide these backup features to you. It is stored only on your own computer and in your own Google Drive. It is not sold, not shared with anyone, not used for advertising, and not used to develop, improve or train any artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models. No person reads it.

SitCraft's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • Disconnect Google Drive at any time in SitCraft under Versions & backup. This revokes SitCraft's access. You can also remove access from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Backup files already in your Drive stay there until you delete them.
  • Remove your API key in App settings.
  • Delete your data by deleting the SitCraft folder in your Documents folder and, if you used Drive backup, the backup folder in your Google Drive.

Children

SitCraft is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
